Cookies

There are none. That is the entire policy, but here is the detail behind it.

There is no banner because there is nothing to consent to

This site sets no cookies, first-party or otherwise. It also uses no local storage, no session storage and no other means of leaving anything behind on your device. Nothing here needs your consent, so nothing interrupts you to ask for it.

No analytics either

We do not measure visitors. There is no tag manager, no pixel, no heatmap and no third-party analytics of any kind. We genuinely do not know how many people read this page.

The apps menu

The grid button at the right of the menu loads a small script from a Lightmorphic address to draw the list of our other apps and their icons. We checked it against this policy: it sets no cookies, writes nothing to your browser's storage, and sends nothing anywhere. It only draws a menu.

The app itself

Castmorphic Subtitles is a desktop app rather than a website, so cookies do not arise. It keeps the subtitle files you save and a copy of yt-dlp, both on your own machine, and nothing else.

Asking about this

If you think you have found something on this site that behaves like a cookie, we would like to know. Write to privacy@lightmorphic.com and we will look and tell you what we find.
